Three ways to read a philosophy book

Studying philosophy can lead to a deep understanding of human thoughts and ideas. A person who studies philosophy is called a philosopher. A philosopher’s work may include logic, ethics, religion and other topics related to philosophy. Reading a philosophy book can help you understand the thoughts of philosophers and develop your own philosophical ideas.

A philosopher’s book may be difficult to understand since the ideas are abstract. For example, consider the line from the introduction to Friedrich Nietzsche’s ‘Thus Spoke Zarathustra’: ‘Gods are dead-let there beordes!’-where ‘there are’ is a sign of punctuation indicating the end of a sentence. This is an example of the poetic language used in philosophical discourse. While poetry and philosophical theories are not intended for lay audiences, those who study philosophy should find it rewarding to learn and convey their thoughts in creative language.

You can approach a philosophy book in several ways, from a strict academic point of seeking to understand the book to a more spiritual approach to learning from the book. When reading a philosophy book, you may choose one of two approaches: either a more strictly academic approach or a more spiritual one. The word academic comes from the Latin word ‘academicus,’ which means ‘of or pertaining to the university.’ In other words, an academic approach is serious and methodical- it involves gaining knowledge through study and thought rather than through direct experience. On the other hand, the word spiritual comes from the Latin word ‘spiritus,’ which means ‘breath.’ Thus, when referring to someone’s soul or life essence, ‘spiritual’ has come to mean something like ‘connected with or indicative of life.’ Taken together, these meanings point toward two distinct ways for people to approach studying philosophy.

If you are interested in learning more about a particular philosopher’s work, you can find out information about him or her by looking at the book’s introduction. For example, if you are interested in reading about Socrates’ life and philosophy, look at the introduction to Plato’s ‘Laws.’ It will provide you with basic facts about Plato’s teacher Socrates and about Plato himself-such as when he was born and where he lived. It will also tell you about Plato’s intentions for writing this work and what type of characters appear in it (e.g., philosophers or politicians). Moreover, since many works of philosophy were written during ancient Greece, it is helpful when reading them to know something about that culture as well as about the author writing under those circumstances.

Philosophy is a way of thinking that can greatly influence our lives-as such, it is an important subject that merits our serious consideration. Philosophy has much to offer us in terms of guiding our thoughts toward greater truth and moral goodness. When reading a philosophy book, consider how you want to approach it so that you can best learn from it. Consider adopting one of two approaches: either a more strictly academic one or a more spiritual one reserved for when you feel motivated enough to absorb deeper wisdom from a book.
